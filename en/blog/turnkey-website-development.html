Turnkey Website Development: What's Included, Timeline & Pricing

July 25, 2026 · 10 min read

"Turnkey website" is a phrase every entrepreneur has heard. But what exactly is included in this service? How long does it take? And how do you avoid overpaying? Let's break it down.

What is a "turnkey website"?

It's full development: from idea to launch. You get a ready-to-use website — with design, development, content, SEO, hosting and domain. You don't need to figure out technical details — the studio handles everything.

Analogy: a "turnkey website" is like a "turnkey car." You don't buy the engine separately, the body separately, and wheels separately. You get a ready car, sit down and drive.

What's included in turnkey website development

  • Analytics & requirements — we study your business, audience, competitors. Create technical requirements.
  • Design — custom layout for your brand. No templates, no builders.
  • Development — responsive across all devices: mobile, tablet, desktop.
  • Content — we upload texts, photos, catalogs, prices.
  • SEO — meta tags, structure, Schema.org, sitemap, robots.txt.
  • SSL certificate — secure HTTPS connection — free.
  • Hosting for 1 year — reliable server placement.
  • Domain for 1 year — your own internet address.
  • Contact form — collect inquiries from visitors.
  • Analytics — Yandex.Metrika and Google Analytics — setup and integration.
  • AI chat — chatbot for 24/7 visitor support.
  • Training — we show you how to manage the site, add content.
  • Support — free edits for the first month.

Step-by-Step Website Creation Process

Step 1: Brief & Analytics (1-2 days)

You fill out a brief: describe your business, target audience, competitors. We analyze the market and create requirements.

Step 2: Design (3-7 days)

We create a unique layout. You see the design before development — changes are possible. Usually 2-3 iterations.

Step 3: Development (3-7 days)

Turn design into working code. Responsive across all devices. Tested in different browsers.

Step 4: Content (2-5 days)

Upload texts, photos, catalogs. If you don't have content — we create it (for additional fee).

Step 5: SEO & Analytics (1-2 days)

Configure meta tags, sitemap, Schema.org. Connect Yandex.Metrika and Google Analytics.

Step 6: Launch (1 day)

Transfer to hosting, connect domain, verify SSL. The site is ready!

How to Choose a Developer

What to look for:

  • Portfolio — do they have completed projects? Look at quality, not quantity.
  • Reviews — what do clients say? Look for real reviews, not paid ones.
  • Contract — willing to sign one? No contract = high risk.
  • Timeline — promising "1 day"? Red flag.
  • Price — too cheap = poor quality. Market average is $250-600 for a landing page.
  • Support — what happens after launch? Who will update?

Common Mistakes When Ordering a Website

  • Chasing price — the cheapest option usually costs more when you need to redo it.
  • No requirements — without technical requirements, the result is unpredictable.
  • Content not ready — texts and photos delay the project by weeks.
  • Want "everything at once" — the more complex the site, the longer the timeline. Be realistic.
  • No support budget — the site needs updates after launch.
