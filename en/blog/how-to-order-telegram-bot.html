How to Order a Telegram Bot: Step-by-Step Guide

July 24, 2026 · 7 min read

A Telegram bot is a powerful business tool: it answers customer questions 24/7, collects leads, and automates sales. But how do you order a bot that actually solves problems instead of becoming another dead project?

Step 1: Define the Task

1

What problem does the bot solve?

Before ordering a bot, answer: what specific problem will it solve? Here are common use cases:

  • Customer support — bot answers frequently asked questions (prices, hours, addresses)
  • Lead collection — bot accepts orders and forwards them to you
  • Sales automation — bot sends invoices, accepts payments
  • Mailings — bot sends news and promotions
  • Appointment booking — bot coordinates schedules

Without a clear task, the bot becomes a toy that doesn't bring business value.

Step 2: Choose the Bot Type

2

What kind of bot do you need?

  • Simple bot — buttons, menus, Q&A responses. Good for business cards and FAQ.
  • Integration bot — connects to CRM, payment system, database.
  • AI bot — understands natural language, responds like a live manager. Good for support and consultations.

A simple bot is cheaper, but an AI bot solves more tasks and scales better.

Step 3: Prepare Technical Requirements

3

What should be in the requirements?

You don't need a 50-page document. Just answer these questions:

  1. Goal — one or two sentences (e.g., "Accept food delivery orders")
  2. Audience — who will use it (customers, employees, partners)
  3. Features — list of what the bot should do
  4. Design — brand colors, logo, style preferences
  5. Integrations — CRM, payment, database connections needed
  6. Examples — show 2-3 bots you like

Step 4: Choose a Developer

4

Where to look and what to check?

  • Studio — guarantee, contract, support. More expensive but reliable.
  • Freelancer — cheaper but higher risk of disappearing.
  • Telegram — many specialists in niche channels.

What to check:

  • Portfolio — do they have completed bots?
  • Reviews — what do other clients say?
  • Contract — are they willing to sign one?
  • Support — what happens after launch?

Step 5: Discuss Cost and Timeline

5

How much does a Telegram bot cost?

  • Simple bot — from $130, 3-5 days
  • AI bot — from $250, 5-10 days
  • Integration bot — from $400, 2-4 weeks

Important: ask what's included in the price. Many quote only development costs but forget hosting ($5-15/mo) and support.

Step 6: Test the Finished Bot

6

What to check before accepting delivery?

  1. All features — test every scenario
  2. Errors — try entering invalid data
  3. Speed — does the bot respond quickly?
  4. Design — buttons, emojis, formatting
  5. Mobile — is everything readable on phone?

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a Telegram bot cost?

From $130 for a basic bot. With AI — from $250. With CRM integration — from $400.

How long does it take to build a Telegram bot?

Basic bot: 3-5 days. AI bot: 5-10 days. Complex bot with integrations: 2-4 weeks.

What tasks can a Telegram bot solve?

Customer support, lead collection, sales automation, mailings, FAQ, appointment booking.

Do I need a server for the bot?

No, the bot runs on the developer's server. You only pay for hosting — from $5/month.
