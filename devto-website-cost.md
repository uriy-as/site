# How Much Does a Website Actually Cost in 2026? Real Numbers, No Agency Fluff

"Hi, how much does a website cost?" is the single most common message any web studio, freelancer, or agency receives. If you're a developer, you've heard it. If you're a client, you've probably googled it and gotten answers ranging from "$50 with Wix" to "$50,000 enterprise".

The truth is: **there is no single number**, and anyone who gives you one before understanding your project is lying. But I can give you *real ranges* for 2026, and — more importantly — the **hidden recurring costs** that 90% of quotes conveniently forget to mention.

## Website Types and Their Real Cost in 2026

| Type | Timeline | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Landing page | 5–7 days | from $250 |
| Business card site | 7–10 days | from $350 |
| Corporate website | 2–3 weeks | from $600 |
| Online store | 3–6 weeks | from $800 |
| Web app / SaaS | 1–3 months | from $2000 |

These are mid-market studio prices, not bottom-of-the-barrel freelancer rates and not enterprise retainers.

## What's Actually Included in That Price

A "from $250" quote is meaningless unless you know what's inside. A complete package should include:

- **Design** — custom layout for your brand, not a stock template
- **Development** — responsive across mobile, tablet, desktop
- **SEO** — meta tags, structure, Schema.org, sitemap
- **SSL certificate** — secure HTTPS
- **Hosting for 1 year**
- **Domain for 1 year**
- **Contact form**
- **Analytics** — Yandex.Metrika and Google Analytics

If a quote is cheap *and* doesn't list these, ask what's missing.

## What Drives the Price Up

### 1. Number of pages
A landing page is one page. A corporate site can be 10–30 pages. Every page is design + content + development hours.

### 2. Design
Template design is cheap but looks like ten thousand other sites. Custom design costs more but is what actually differentiates you.

### 3. Functionality
A contact form is baseline. A calculator, client dashboard, or CRM integration? That's where the invoice grows.

### 4. Content
No texts or photos? Someone has to create them. Copywriting, photography, graphics all add up fast.

### 5. Ongoing SEO
Basic on-page SEO is usually included. Active promotion/search ranking is a separate service, typically from $70/month.

## The Hidden Costs Nobody Tells You About

Here's the part even experienced clients miss — the **recurring costs after launch**:

- **Hosting** — from $5–20/month (after the first free year expires)
- **Domain renewal** — from $10–15/year
- **Maintenance** — from $50/month for updates, backups, and fixes
- **Premium SSL** — often free, but premium certs run from $50/year

A "$250 landing page" can quietly become a "$40/month forever" commitment. That's not a scam — that's how the web works. The best studios are upfront about it.

## Freelancer vs Studio vs Website Builder

| Parameter | Freelancer | Studio | Builder (Wix/Tilda) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Landing page cost | $100–300 | $250–600 | $0–30/mo |
| Design quality | Depends on freelancer | Guaranteed | Template-based |
| SEO | Basic | Full | Limited |
| Support | Often disappears | Guaranteed | DIY |
| Website ownership | Yes | Yes | No (you rent it) |

The build-it-yourself option isn't "free" — it's a subscription where you never own the site and you do all the SEO work yourself.

## How to Actually Save Money

- **Prepare content in advance** — texts, photos, logo. This removes the biggest variable cost.
- **Decide on features up front** — don't order a dashboard you'll never open.
- **Get a landing page if that's all you need** — most small businesses don't need 20 pages.
- **Don't automatically pick the cheapest** — cheap = redoing = double the cost.

## My Recommendation

For most small businesses, a landing page or business card site in the **$250–400** range covers 90% of real needs: presenting the service, collecting leads, and basic SEO.

The golden rule: **don't chase the lowest price.** Paid once and got a working site beats paid three times to fix a broken one.
