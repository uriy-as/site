# Telegram Bots for Business: 7 Use Cases With Real Prices (2026)

Most "chatbot" pitches are vague. Here are the seven scenarios we actually build, what each one does, what it costs in 2026, and how quickly it pays for itself.

The short version: a Telegram bot is the cheapest sales and support channel you can add this year, because your customers already live in the app and your competitors are still waiting for a callback.

## 1. Lead capture bot — from $800

Collects name, phone, and the task, qualifies it with 3–5 questions, and sends a ready-to-work lead to your manager in Telegram or CRM.

**Why it works:** the form on your site loses 60–80% of the people who start filling it. A bot in a chat asks three questions and finishes the job.

**Payback:** usually 1–2 months, or the cost of one advertisement.

## 2. Appointment booking bot — from $700

Calendar, reminders, rescheduling, and a reminder to the manager 30 minutes before the meeting. No more "did you confirm?" calls.

**Payback:** one saved no-show per week pays for it.

## 3. Shop in Telegram — from $1,200

Catalog, cart, payment, delivery, and order status inside the messenger. No website, no app install, no friction.

**When it makes sense:** when your customers are already in Telegram and your average check is small enough to buy without a card form.

**Payback:** 3–6 months for a store with 30+ orders per month.

## 4. AI consultant on your knowledge base — from $1,400

Answers questions about your product, prices, delivery terms, and warranty using your own documents. Falls back to a human when it is not sure.

**Payback:** 2–4 months — it removes the repetitive half of support work.

## 5. Sales automation bot — from $1,800

Qualifies the lead, offers options, prepares a quote, reminds about an unpaid invoice, and hands the hot deal to a human with the full context.

**Key detail:** the bot never closes a deal alone. It removes the manual part so the seller talks only to people who are ready.

## 6. Listing and price monitor — from $800

Watches marketplaces, boards, and competitor prices, and sends a Telegram alert when something matches your criteria. We built one for a car dealer and one for a real estate agency.

**Payback:** one extra deal per month.

## 7. Internal assistant for the team — from $600

Answers staff questions from the company knowledge base: how to file a vacation request, where the scripts are, what the warranty policy says.

**Payback:** hard to measure, but onboarding and repeat questions shrink visibly within a month.

## What really costs money

| Factor | Effect on price |
|---|---|
| Number of scenarios (branches) | Each extra flow adds $300–800 |
| CRM / payment / delivery integration | +$300–700 per system |
| AI consultant with a knowledge base | +$400–600 |
| Custom design and branding | +$200–500 |
| Urgent delivery (under 5 days) | +20–30% |

## How to start without wasting money

1. **Pick one scenario, not a "Telegram solution".** Most projects fail because they try to do everything at once.
2. **Start with the cheapest version that measures something.** We launch with a lead-capture bot and analytics, then extend.
3. **Define the handover to a human.** A bot that never passes a conversation to a person gets deleted by staff.
4. **Write down the fallback answers.** If a bot improvises prices, you will have a problem.
